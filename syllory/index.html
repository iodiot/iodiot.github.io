Private by design

Your reading stays yours.

Imported books, reading position, and preferences stay in the local library on this Mac. When a supported Mac running macOS 26 or later has Apple Intelligence available, selected text and nearby context are processed by Apple’s on-device Foundation Models—not by a Syllory server.
