A calm, customizable EPUB and FB2 reader for macOS.

Import EPUB and FB2 books from your Mac. Shape the type, spacing, theme, and page layout to fit each book. Your library and reading place stay on this Mac. On supported Macs, use on-device AI to explain or translate a selected passage.

01 · Local AI

Make sense of a whole paragraph.
Translate it in context.

Select a word, sentence, or full paragraph in the reader. Ask Syllory to explain the passage as a whole, or choose from 32 translation language options and translate with the surrounding text in mind.

Runs with Apple Foundation Models when Apple Intelligence is available on a supported Mac with macOS 26 or later.

02 Made for the way you read

05 · Private by design

Your reading stays yours.

Imported books, reading position, and preferences stay in the local library on this Mac. When Apple Intelligence is available on a supported Mac running macOS 26 or later, selected text and nearby context are processed by Apple’s on-device Foundation Models. Syllory does not send them to its own server.
