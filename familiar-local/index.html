01 · On-device AI

Think with a model that runs on your Mac.

Familiar sends prompts to Apple Foundation Models through a small local service. No hosted AI provider receives the chat. The model context stays in memory while Familiar is running.

04 Starts local. Stays local.

Familiar is a native shell around a small Swift service and a local web chat.

Open the app

Familiar starts its helper and opens the chat in a native window.

Chat on this Mac

The helper listens only at 127.0.0.1, so it is not exposed to other devices on your network.

Return when you like

Chat history stays in local browser storage. Model context resets when the helper stops.

05 · Private by design

Your chats stay on this Mac.

Familiar does not send prompts to a hosted AI service. The helper accepts connections only from this Mac, Apple Foundation Models runs on-device, and chat history stays in browser storage on this Mac. Familiar has no account, ads, or analytics.
