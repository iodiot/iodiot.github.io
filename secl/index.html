04 · GPU from first principles

No engine, just shaders: voxels, rays, fluids

GpuVoxels

Ray casting a 64×64×64 voxel grid stored as an OpenGL 3D texture; every pixel walks the grid block by block until it hits.

github.com/iodiot/GpuVoxels

GpuRayTracer

Live ray tracing in a single shader: two light bounces between spheres, soft shadows from an area light.

github.com/iodiot/GpuRayTracer

GpuFluid

Navier–Stokes on the GPU: advection, buoyancy, Jacobi pressure solve as shader passes; vorticity confinement, 512 grid.

github.com/iodiot/GpuFluid

07 · Teaching WebGPU

“Three.js from Scratch”: a free series for frontend devs

Six parts so far, from a first lit cube to shaders, WebGPU and post-processing. Diagrams, real screenshots and a live demo in every part.

Part 5 · WebGPU and TSL
